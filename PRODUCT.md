# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Developers and their colleagues who work with MongoDB every day: browsing collections, querying, editing documents, building aggregations, moving data between environments (local, staging, production). They switch between many connections and expect to always know which database they are touching.

## Product Purpose
odin-db is a free, open source desktop client for MongoDB. It aims for MongoDB Compass's simplicity and visual polish together with Studio 3T's depth (copy/paste of collections and databases, imports/exports, backups, shell, compare, query tools). Success: people prefer it to both for daily work and fall in love with how simple it is to use.

## Brand Personality
Simple, polished, fast. Calm and confident; the interface disappears into the task. Compact without feeling cramped, customizable without being fiddly.

## Anti-references
- Studio 3T's dense, toolbar-heavy, enterprise look: powerful but cluttered and dated.
- Generic admin dashboards: card grids, hero metrics, decorative gradients.
- Anything that hides the connection's identity (environment color) or makes production look like development.

## Design Principles
1. Compass-level simplicity: the common task is one obvious click; advanced power is one step away, never in the way.
2. Always know where you are: connection color, breadcrumbs and the tree tell you which server and collection you are on.
3. Standard affordances: shadcn/ui components, platform shortcuts (Cmd on macOS, Ctrl elsewhere), familiar patterns over invented ones.
4. Dense but calm: compact rows and tight spacing, with restraint in color and decoration.
5. Make it yours: themes, accent, density and fonts are adjustable.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 text contrast in light and dark themes, full keyboard navigation (tree, grid, dialogs), visible focus, reduced-motion respected, colors never the only signal (labels accompany environment colors).
